# Boardy

> Boardy is an AI Superconnector designed to facilitate meaningful introductions. Boardy invites people to message it to discuss who they want to meet.

Boardy's public terms say that public LinkedIn profile information may be used to facilitate introductions. Contact information beyond what is publicly available is shared only after both parties have explicitly opted in to connect.

Boardy's public privacy policy explains how personal information is collected, used, and protected, including the available choices for marketing communications, connected calendars, and data requests.
